The story behind Tower Lady Pictures

We make independent short films in Poland. We’re drawn to thrillers and to what happens when suspense meets dark drama or the absurd.

A name from Szamotuły

Our name comes from the legend of Halszka. The tower linked to her story still stands in Szamotuły, the filmmaker’s hometown. It’s the kind of detail we’re drawn to in films: a familiar place that feels different once you know the story behind it.

How we work

We make these films after hours. We work with a small crew, talk through the script and build the sets together. A short that runs for a few minutes can take months to prepare. That time gives us room to decide what the audience needs to see and what can be left to their imagination.

What we’re exploring

For new projects, shooting with actors remains the starting point. We’re exploring where digital tools, including AI, can extend what we make on set, whether that means expanding a set or developing a new shot from frames we’ve filmed. The choice depends on what the scene needs.
